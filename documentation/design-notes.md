# V6 Engine CAD Assembly — Design Notes

## Project Purpose

This project was completed as a CAD recreation and learning exercise in Autodesk Fusion 360.

The primary goal was to improve my understanding of complex mechanical assemblies, component relationships, engine structure, and mechanical motion.

The engine architecture was recreated using a YouTube reference and was not developed as an original engine concept.

## Modelling Approach

The project was developed progressively by modelling and organizing individual engine components before integrating them into the complete assembly.

Major areas of focus included:

- Engine block geometry
- Crankshaft and piston arrangement
- Cylinder heads
- Camshaft and valve-related components
- Intake and exhaust systems
- Supporting engine components
- Assembly organization
- Component positioning and relationships

## Assembly Development

As the project grew, managing the assembly became an important part of the workflow.

Components were organized separately in Fusion 360 so that their relationships could be understood and managed more clearly.

The final assembly includes multiple mechanical and airflow-related components integrated into one engine model.

## Crankshaft–Piston Motion

One of the most useful parts of the project was studying the relationship between crankshaft rotation and piston movement.

The assembly demonstrates how rotational movement of the crankshaft produces reciprocating motion of the pistons.

A motion demonstration is available in:

`motion/crankshaft_pistons_motion.mp4`

## Exploded View

An exploded assembly view was created to better visualize how the major engine components are positioned relative to one another.

This also helped me understand the engine as a collection of interacting subassemblies rather than only as a finished external model.

## Key Learning Outcomes

Through this project, I improved my understanding of:

- Complex CAD assembly workflow
- Mechanical component relationships
- Component organization in Fusion 360
- Crankshaft and piston kinematics
- Exploded assembly presentation
- Managing a large multi-component CAD project
- Presenting engineering work through technical documentation
